# Teacha social preview

`teacha-preview.jpg` is a 1200 × 630 preview composed from repository-owned
`assets/logo/teacha-wordmark.svg` and cropped drink photographs from the existing
`assets/menu/` JPG menu boards. Typography uses the existing Nunito font;
its license remains in `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`.

The reproducible layout and WebP conversions are in `scripts/build-images.mjs`.
No new stock imagery or AI-generated drink photographs were introduced.
